The 4-Stage Autonomous Pipeline

How Job Hunter automates your search overhead while keeping you in total control

Stage 01

Scout ATS Boards

Directly queries public ATS endpoints from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, BambooHR, Recruitee, Breezy HR, and Pinpoint at high concurrency.

Stage 02

Stealth Pre-Filter

Deterministic regex matching removes ~99% of junk titles, out-of-region postings, and stale listings with zero API waste.

Stage 03

Precision Fit Scoring

Surviving roles are scored (0.0–10.0) against your uploaded resume and criteria with detailed match reasoning.

Stage 04

Application Kits & Alerts

Top matches (7.0+) are compiled into a responsive HTML briefing with custom outreach notes and tailored resume highlights.
